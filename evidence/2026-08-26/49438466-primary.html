About
the Report

In January 2026, MIT charged the Committee on AI Use in Teaching, Learning, and Research Training, to assess how instructors and students are currently using generative AI, identify innovations it allows and challenges it presents in teaching and assessment, and propose an AI use policy.

This page presents the committee’s final report, along with related resources.
